# PixelPen

**Pixels of Thought, Pens of Change**

A publishing home for writers and readers who want depth without the noise. Create an account, write your story, and explore articles from people who still care about good words.

---

## What you can do

**Create your account**  
Sign in with Google, GitHub, or Facebook. You’ll get a public profile and a place that feels like yours.

**Write blogs**  
Open the editor and publish long-form posts with rich formatting — headings, images, code, tables, and more. Add them to categories so readers can find you.

**Read articles**  
Browse featured stories, explore by topic, search for what interests you, and leave comments. Every piece is built for focused reading, not endless scrolling past ads.

**Make it yours**  
Update your profile, track your writing stats, and switch between light and dark mode whenever you like.

---

## Why PixelPen — and why not just Medium?

Medium is huge. That’s the problem. Algorithms, paywalls, and a feed that often feels more like a magazine stand than a conversation with a writer.

PixelPen is built for the opposite experience:

| | Medium | PixelPen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reading | Paywalls & recommendation noise | Clean, open reading — content first |
| AI | Generic platform tools | **Per-article summaries** and a **chatbot that knows the piece you’re reading** |
| Design | Familiar, crowded | Modern, distraction-free UI with light & dark themes |
| Community | Follow the algorithm | Profiles, comments, and real engagement around each story |
| Focus | Monetize attention | Amplify the writing |

### What makes us unique

**AI that stays with the article**  
Every post can be summarized in one click. Want to go deeper? Open the in-article chatbot and ask questions about *that* story — not the whole internet.

**A canvas, not a feed**  
The interface is designed so your words (or the ones you’re reading) stay front and center. No clutter fighting for your attention.

**Real engagement**  
Public profiles, comments, and writing stats help you grow an audience that actually showed up for your work — not a trending carousel.

**Built for storytellers**  
Whether it’s your first draft or your hundredth, PixelPen is a sanctuary for thoughtful writing: culture, coding, travel, food, style, and whatever else you need to say.
